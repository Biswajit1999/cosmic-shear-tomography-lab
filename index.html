Three ideas before the graph

What are we actually measuring?

01

Every galaxy is noisy

Its original shape is unknown. One stretched galaxy proves nothing, so astronomers compare many galaxy pairs.

02

ξ+ measures agreement

Positive ξ+ means nearby galaxy shapes share a preferred alignment more often than random orientations would produce.

03

Tomography adds depth

KiDS divides sources into five redshift bins. Higher bins look through more intervening structure and usually accumulate more lensing.

What does “zero shear” mean?

It is a deliberately simple null hypothesis: all nine ξ+ values are zero. Rejecting it shows correlated galaxy shapes in this data vector. It does not by itself estimate dark-matter density or S8.

What this laboratory does—and does not do.

Directly observed here

Fifteen ξ+ tomographic vectors, their angular bins, the released covariance blocks and the five KiDS source-redshift distributions.

Calculated here

A transparent χ² test of ξ+ = 0 for one selected bin pair. The complete covariance block is used; points are not treated as independent.

Not claimed here

No S8 posterior, cosmological fit, intrinsic-alignment inference or baryonic-feedback constraint. Those require the full likelihood and theory pipeline.
